# Interview Log

Source: survey on finding offices and rooms at MSU-IIT. Respondents are identified only by Respondent ID and Submission ID. Names, emails, and social links are removed for privacy purposes.

Submissions: 32. Two have no answers beyond consent and one has demographics only, so 29 answered the core questions. The feature question appears only in the later version of the form, so feature counts use 23 respondents.

## Key Interview Insights

### Key findings

| Finding | Evidence | Why it matters for the app |
|---|---|---|
| Strong demand | Average intent to use is 4.7 out of 5. 21 of 29 answered 5 and only 1 answered 3. | Core idea is validated. Nobody scored below 3. |
| Finding places is a moderate pain | Average problem rating is 3.5 out of 5 (n=23). 8 rated 4 and 3 rated 5. | Pain is real but not extreme. Speed and low effort will decide adoption. |
| Frustration and wrong turns are the main costs | 14 of 29 felt frustrated or stressed. 14 went to the wrong place first. Only 3 reported being late, missing a deadline, or giving up. | Design for fewer wrong turns and less stress, not only for saving time. |
| People rely on other people | 16 asked a classmate or org mate, 15 walked around, 9 asked staff, 7 asked a guard. Only 4 read a posted directory. | Posted directories are weak today. The app should replace asking around. |
| Most searches are short but some are long | 12 took 5 to 10 minutes. 6 took 2 to 5 minutes. 6 took more than 10 minutes, with 1 over 30 minutes. | Long tail cases are likely first time visitors or hidden offices. |
| Admin building is the pain point | Admin was named 5 times, the most of any building. CASS and CSM followed with 4 each. | Map and index the Admin building first, then CASS and CSM. |
| First years and student organizations need it most | 11 of 30 are first years and 19 are in an organization. 3 respondents said it helps first years most. | Pilot with first years and org officers who run errands. |
| Org errands mix finding and waiting | Of 16 who run errands, 9 said time splits half finding and half waiting. 2 said mostly finding and 4 said mostly waiting. | Maps solve only part of the delay. Office hours and process info help the rest. |
| Trust is not fragile | Only 1 of 29 would stop using it after a wrong room. 23 would report it and 16 would double check. | A report wrong info button is worth building. Users will help fix data. |
| Adoption barriers | 5 do not want to open another app or site. 12 said they rarely need to find places. | Keep it web based, fast, and linked from MyIIT if possible. Offline use was requested. |

### Features requested (n=23)

| Feature | Votes | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Search an office by name | 20 | 87% |
| Browse offices by building and floor | 19 | 83% |
| Floor plan with the room highlighted | 17 | 74% |
| Search by room number | 17 | 74% |
| Office hours | 15 | 65% |
| Report wrong or outdated information | 5 | 22% |

Write in requests: staff directory with office heads and photos, nearest trash bin, facility availability for org activities, offline saved maps, real time GPS, a campus wide overview map, search keywords for offices, and how to book rooms.

### Suggested MVP priorities

| Priority | Build | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Search by office name and room number | Search by office name was chosen by 20 of 23 respondents and search by room number by 17 of 23. These are the quickest ways to reach a known destination. |
| 2 | Building and floor browser with highlighted floor plans | Browse by building and floor was chosen by 19 of 23 respondents and the highlighted floor plan by 17 of 23. Several respondents also asked for a map of each building. |
| 3 | Campus overview map with building locations | Several respondents asked for a campus wide map and a Google Maps style view. |
| 4 | Office hours and head of office | Office hours was chosen by 15 of 23 respondents. One respondent also asked for office heads and photos. |
| 5 | Report wrong info button | Strong stated willingness to report. Keeps data accurate. |
| 6 | Offline mode | Requested directly and lowers the another app barrier. |
